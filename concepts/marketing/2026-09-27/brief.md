# HushMessenger visual brief

The Hush projects use one dark hero at the top of the README, a separate square mark, and blue or cyan light against navy. HushMessenger uses conversation cards instead of Hushfacebook's feed cards. The artwork has no version number, app screenshot or claim about patches that have not shipped.

## Five mark directions

1. **Minimal icon:** Create a logo for HushMessenger, a Morphe patch source for Facebook Messenger. Draw one flat white H with a cyan link across its center. Use thick, SVG-friendly edges that remain clear at 16 px on both light and dark surfaces. No text, gradient or background. Deliver a PNG with an RGBA channel and alpha 0 everywhere outside the glyph.
2. **App icon, selected:** Create an app mark for HushMessenger, a Morphe patch source for Facebook Messenger. Build a white geometric H from two subtle conversation cards inside a cobalt rounded square tile. Add a small cyan bridge so it differs from Hushfacebook's plain H. Keep the silhouette crisp at 64 px and readable against light and dark surfaces. No text. Deliver a PNG with RGBA and alpha 0 outside the tile.
3. **Wordmark:** Create a wordmark for HushMessenger, a Morphe patch source for Facebook Messenger. Set HUSHMESSENGER in a wide geometric sans serif with a short cyan underline. Use clean vector-friendly letter forms, no shadows and enough contrast for both light and dark placement. No other text or background. Deliver a PNG with RGBA and alpha 0 outside the letters and underline.
4. **Emblem:** Create an emblem for HushMessenger, a Morphe patch source for Facebook Messenger. Frame a compact white H with two offset blue message outlines. Keep the badge legible at 128 px, with simple SVG-friendly geometry and contrast on light and dark surfaces. No text or background. Deliver a PNG with RGBA and alpha 0 outside the emblem.
5. **Abstract:** Create an abstract mark for HushMessenger, a Morphe patch source for Facebook Messenger. Show two blue message shapes moving into alignment around a bright cyan center line. Use a small number of crisp, vector-friendly shapes that work on light and dark surfaces. No text, gradient or background. Deliver a PNG with RGBA and alpha 0 outside the form.
